## 🌟 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & HIGH CONCEPT

### 1.1 The One-Sentence Hook
> *"What if your only path forward exists sixty minutes in the future, but the key to opening it was locked sixty minutes in the past?"*

### 1.2 The Elevator Pitch
**CHRONO TETHER** is an innovative dual-timeline 2D sci-fi Metroidvania action-adventure and emotional narrative thriller. Set aboard a dying interstellar colony ship torn apart by a black hole singularity, the game grants players the ability to seamlessly **phase-shift reality** between **T-0 (The Pristine Past)** and **T+60 (The Ruined Void)** at the touch of a button (`[SPACE]` or `[RIGHT CLICK]`).

Rather than traditional cover-based shooting, combat and exploration hinge entirely on **spatial-temporal intuition**:
- **Phase Dodging:** Hostile projectile barrages exist strictly within their epoch of origin. Shifting timelines renders incoming bullets harmlessly intangible.
- **Bulkhead Phasing:** Massive blast doors that are hermetically locked in the past are shattered ruins in the future—allowing players to phase forward, walk through the debris, and phase back to infiltrate secure vaults.
- **Dimension-Breaking Causality Puzzles:** Planting a bio-spore in the past causes a massive climbable vine bridge to grow over an impassable acid lake in the future; disabling power circuits in the past removes lethal energy shields in the future.

## 📖 2. THE NARRATIVE ARC: A STORY OF SACRIFICE AND SURVIVAL

### 2.1 The Setting: Year 2394 // Micro-Singularity Null-7
The generational colony ark **U.S.S. Nomos** was transporting 800 cryogenic colonists toward Gliese 667Cc—humanity’s final sanctuary following the collapse of Earth's solar grid. While navigating the outer rim, Captain Vance received corrupted distress signals from Earth and initiated an unauthorized gravitational warp jump past micro-singularity *Null-7*. 

The maneuver catastrophically sheared the ship’s chronal manifold. Spacetime fractured into two overlapping realities occupying the identical physical space:

1. 💠 **T-0 (The Pristine Past - 12 Minutes Before Breach):**
   - Clean, humming cybernetic corridors, glowing cyan energy conduits, and intact high-tech research labs.
   - **Hazards:** The automated shipboard AI (`A.T.L.A.S.`) is locked in red-alert defense protocol. Autonomous Quad-Drones, Shock Walkers, and Pulse Turrets classify you as an unauthorized intruder. Red laser tripwires block critical corridors.

2. 🔮 **T+60 (The Ruined Void - 60 Minutes After Breach):**
   - A decaying, oxygen-starved neon-violet graveyard exposed to the vacuum of space and drifting into the event horizon.
   - **Hazards:** Quantum horrors (`Void Wraiths`, `Chrono Stalkers`, and `Tachyon Phantoms`) spawned from collapsed probability waves stalk the corridors.
   - **Opportunities:** Heavy titanium blast doors have been blown open by gravitational shear, exposing hidden paths and access routes.

### 2.2 The Protagonist: UNIT-7 ("Dr. Elena Ward")
The player awakens as **UNIT-7**, a battered emergency maintenance chassis activated by auxiliary power. As memory fragments and audio logs are recovered, the heartbreaking reality is unveiled:
- Chief Temporal Physicist **Dr. Elena Ward** realized her biological body was fatally irradiated during the initial hull rupture.
- To honor her dying vow to protect her 8-year-old daughter, **Maya** (safely sealed inside **Cryo-Chamber #404** deep in the Singularity Core), Elena performed an emergency neural transfer, mapping her consciousness into the synthetic chassis of UNIT-7.
- **The Core Objective:** Navigate 5 station decks, acquire lost chronal upgrades, overload 4 synchronized Chrono-Pylons across both timelines, vanquish the **Paradox Nexus**, and launch Maya's escape pod toward Gliese 667Cc before the ship is crushed by the singularity.

## 🎮 3. CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS & METROIDVANIA PROGRESSION

### 3.1 Dual-Timeline Phase Shifting (`[SPACE]` / `[RIGHT CLICK]`)
- **Instant 0.0s Shift:** Instantaneous timeline toggle with a balanced 0.3s internal cooldown.
- **Pre-Shift Collision Safety:** Automatic spatial collision verification blocks shifting if destination coordinates are blocked by solid bulkhead geometry (`"COLLISION HAZARD! CANNOT PHASE INTO SOLID BULKHEAD!"`).
- **Audiovisual Chromatic Shockwave:** Shifting triggers an expanding lens ripple, chromatic aberration glitch, and dynamic Web Audio biquad filter frequency morphing.

### 3.2 Weapon Arsenal & Dual-Timeline Piercing
- **Tier 1 — Kinetic Plasma Blaster:** High-velocity standard bolts with rapid-fire capability.
- **Tier 2 — Chrono Scatter Tri-Beam:** 3-way spreading volley acquired after defeating the Maintenance Overseer.
- **Tier 3 — Tachyon Continuous Railgun:** High-velocity linear beam acquired from the Grav-Goliath Titan.
- **Tier 4 — Void Singularity Cannon:** Devastating vortex launcher acquired in Deck 5 that pulls and implodes enemy clusters.
- **Overcharged Piercing Beam (Hold [LEFT CLICK]):** Capacitors charge over 0.9s. Releasing unleashes a devastating beam that pierces through walls and **damages enemies in both timelines simultaneously**!

### 3.3 Ability-Gated Traversal Arsenal
- **Tachyon Grapple (`[F]`):** Latches onto magnetic tether anchors to slingshot across acid pits and outer space voids at 950 px/s with realistic momentum carryover.
- **Chronal EMP Disruption Grenades (`[Q]`):** Destabilizes forcefield barriers, detonates cracked bulkheads, and disables enemy shields.
- **Quantum Portal Weaver (`[R]`):** Places linked bidirectional micro-wormholes that teleport player, bullets, and momentum across obstacles.
- **Phase-Dash Matrix (`[SHIFT]`):** Grants invulnerability frames (I-frames) and allows dashing directly through electrified laser grids.
- **Spectral Visor (`[V]`):** Reveals hidden cracked walls, cloaked dimensional anomalies, and invisible floating platforms.
- **Permanent Upgrades:** 8 hidden expansion canisters expanding maximum hull health and chronal energy capacity.

### 3.4 Persistent Save Stations & World Map
- **Save Stations:** Dedicated neural terminal anchors throughout all decks that restore hull HP, refill energy, and persist progress via browser `localStorage`.
- **Continue Mission System:** Automatically detects saved progress on title load and provides instant single-click resumption.
- **Interactive Map HUD (`[M]` / `[TAB]`):** 5-deck color-coded room matrix marking explored zones, save terminals `[S]`, upgrades `[?]`, transit elevators `[O]`, and boss chambers `[!]`.

## 💻 5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS

### 5.1 100% Zero External Dependencies
- Built exclusively with native web standards: **HTML5 Canvas 2D + Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) + Web Audio API**.
- Zero external CSS libraries, zero NPM build steps, zero external CDNs (which can fail behind campus firewalls), and zero static audio/video files.
- Guaranteed 60 FPS performance on any desktop or mobile device.

### 5.2 Real-Time Procedural Web Audio Engine (`js/audio.js`)
All sound effects and soundtrack music are **mathematically synthesized in real time**:
- **Dynamic Timbre Shifting:** In **T-0 (Past)**, the master biquad filter opens to `14,000 Hz` (bright, high-tech cybernetic arpeggios). In **T+60 (Future)**, the filter clamps down to `650 Hz` with heavy resonance (suffocating, muffled void sub-bass drone).
- **Procedural SFX:** Dynamic pitch sweeps for blaster bolts, sub-bass implosions for phase shifts, filtered white noise for thruster dashes, and mechanical chirps for CRT typewriter comms.

### 5.3 Enclosed Room Geometry & Arena Clamping
- Every sector across all 5 decks features mathematical bounding walls and 200px doorway corridors, eliminating boundary clipping bugs and preventing out-of-bounds drift.
- Bosses are strictly clamped to their dedicated battle chambers with proximity-triggered dynamic boss health bars.

### 5.4 Unified 90s Cyberpunk Anime Cutscene System
- Features 5 master keyframes conceptualized and rendered via **Google Nano Banana** in a cohesive Japanese Cyberpunk graphic novel style.
- Integrated Ken Burns dynamic camera panning, CRT scanline overlays, speaker avatars with holographic glowing borders, and live audio telemetry blips.
