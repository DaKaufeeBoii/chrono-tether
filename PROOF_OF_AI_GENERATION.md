### Entry #015: Deep Root-Cause Diagnosis & Complete Physics Overhaul
- **Timestamp:** 2026-11-23T23:57:00+05:30
- **Context:** User reported "everything is broken." Deep frame-by-frame diagnostic trace revealed multiple root causes:
  1. *Floor Collision Penetration:* Entities were instantiated at Y=0.0, halfway embedded in the static floor collision box, causing continuous floor-shoving jitter and zero velocity.
  2. *Invisible Tether:* `ImmediateMesh` was rendering `PRIMITIVE_LINES`, which is 1-pixel wide in Vulkan Forward+ and invisible from camera distance.
  3. *Deferred Tree Attachment Timing:* Flail and Tether were added deferred, resulting in frame 1 separation and coordinate desync.
  4. *Camera Orientation:* Camera had static rotation rather than dynamic `look_at(target)` tracking.
- **Architectural Overhaul Plan:**
  - Elevate combat to $Y = 1.0$ hover plane across all entities (Drone, Flail, Crates, Switches, Turrets).

  - Upgrade Tether3D to billboarded triangle-strip 3D plasma ribbon (18cm thickness) with glowing energy shader.
  - Fix synchronous instantiation in `Drone3D._ready()`.
  - Configure camera with dynamic look-at tracking.
